Brush Trading ("we", "our", "us") built CDL Test Prep: Practice Exam ("the App") as a free, ad-supported application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up is required
  • No name, email, phone number, or address is collected
  • No location data is collected
  • No photos, contacts, or files are accessed
  • No data is sent to our servers — there are no servers

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Settings preferences — your selected state, theme, and appearance mode
  • Practice progress — which questions you've answered, your accuracy, and exam scores

This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is deleted.
